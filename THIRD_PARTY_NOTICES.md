# Third-party notices

## NotEnoughUpdates item repository

`attribute_shard_rarity.properties`, `attribute_shard_market.properties`, and
`attribute_ability_names.properties` are generated from `constants/attribute_shards.json` in the
NotEnoughUpdates item repository. The first two use revision
`08404d2fd26b7db8707b1899a01f7fb81628c58b`; the ability-name fallback uses revision
`159357789dfc63102d272746bfb1bb3d5b6543b0`.

`attribute_shard_heads.properties` uses the player-head profiles from the repository's
`items/ATTRIBUTE_SHARD_*;1.json` files at revision
`0629f70682a878d510fa7c9c01233092f109ed49`. Eleven missing or duplicated source profiles use
source-themed Mojang texture values documented by MCHeads and Minecraft-Heads so all 320 icons are
player heads and no two rows share a texture.
